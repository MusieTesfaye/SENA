# SENA docs

SENA is a payments network on Aptos. You sign in with Google, hold
stablecoins, and send them to an address, an email address or a phone
number, paying the fee in the coin you send.

## Start here

- **[Get a wallet](/docs/wallets)**: web, browser extension, or MetaMask.
- **[Pay an email or phone](/docs/identities)**: link yours, pay someone else's.
- **[Fees in stablecoins](/docs/fees)**: what you pay, and why it is never more.
- **[Is it safe?](/docs/security)**: what protects your coins, and what does not yet.

## Under the hood

- **[Architecture](/docs/architecture)**: sequencer, Aptos, and the agents between them.
- **[Fraud proofs on Aptos](/docs/fraud-proofs)**: how a wrong block is proven wrong.
- **[Keyless accounts](/docs/keyless)**: Google sign-in as a signature.

## Build

- **[JSON-RPC](/docs/rpc)**: every method the node serves.
- **[Run a node](/docs/run-a-node)**: a watcher, or a whole network.

The source is on GitHub: [the protocol](https://github.com/MusieTesfaye/SENA)
and [the wallets](https://github.com/MusieTesfaye/wallet).
